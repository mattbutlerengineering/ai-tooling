## Triage note

Left at `discovery-log` as the surviving half of a duplicate pair — the InftyAI list of the same
name was SKIPped in this pass as the smaller copy (★253 against this one's ★5.9K).

CC0-1.0 is the right licence for the artifact: a curated list is a document people copy from, and
CC0 removes every question about doing so. That is a real advantage over an Apache-2.0 list, small
as it sounds.

Pushed 2026-05-21 — roughly two and a half months, which for a curated list is worth watching but
not yet stale; the sibling's freshness advantage is the one thing that could invert this pass's
comparison, and it is why the SKIP over there names dormancy as its re-open trigger.

The scope caveat is the same as for every LLMOps reference here: serving and fine-tuning are
operations for people shipping models, not stages of the loop for people shipping software with an
agent's help. A row to keep findable, not a lead to promote.
