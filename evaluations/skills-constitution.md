## What it does

A meta-rule set governing how skills are invoked and composed across agent platforms — a "constitution" skills/agents can defer to when their own instructions conflict or are ambiguous.

## Triage note

Cited as overlapping `agent-skills` and `documentation-and-adrs` by shared "skill
governance" vocabulary, but the job is different — a cross-platform meta-rule for
*how* skills should behave when invoked, not a skill collection or an ADR-writing
skill. Left at discovery-log rather than SKIPped as redundant; a niche but
differentiated governance concept worth a real look if skill-invocation conflicts
become a recurring pain point.
