and install/update selected plugins through a dedicated loader and update utility.

## How we tested it

## Verdict

**SKIP** — GPL-3.0. This routine's license bar admits only permissive (MIT-like) OSS; the
catalog already carries `claude-plugins-official` and `awesome-copilot` for
plugin-discovery/installation workflows under permissive licenses, so there is no gap this
tool alone would fill that justifies taking on copyleft.
