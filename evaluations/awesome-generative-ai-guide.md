## Triage note

Left at `discovery-log`. MIT, ★28.2K, pushed 2026-06-24 — and it is the row that absorbed
`awesome-llm-agents` in this pass rather than the other way round, on breadth and maintenance.

Its distinguishing feature against the other teaching rows is the *research-update* stream: where
`Prompt-Engineering-Guide` is a technique reference and `genai-agents` is a notebook course, this
tracks what is newly published. That is a different job and the one with the shortest shelf life,
which makes cadence the thing to watch — a research digest that stops updating becomes the least
useful row in the section rather than merely a stale one.

Broad gen-AI rather than dev-loop-specific, which is why it stays in Reference.
