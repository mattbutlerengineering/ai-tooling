## Triage note

Left at `discovery-log`, with one observation that is really about the catalog rather than the list.

Its overlap cell names three *skills*; this row is a **list of skills**. Those are not the same kind of thing,
and the same misfiling appeared on `openinference` (a convention layer listed as overlapping four backends)
in the Outer Loop slice. The "Overlaps with" column captures subject matter, and subject matter is not
substitutability — which is exactly why a redundancy pass must read the rows rather than pattern-match the
cell. Two instances in one issue is worth recording.

On its own terms it is a small curated list (MIT, ★1.7K, pushed 2026-06-28) pointing into a cluster this
catalog covers directly and in depth: `ui-ux-pro-max`, `impeccable`, `taste-skill`, `huashu-design`,
`baoyu-design`, `garden-skills`, `open-design`. A reader here does not need the index — they have the
entries. That is an argument about *marginal value*, not redundancy, and it is not strong enough to
eliminate a maintained list.

The lists disposed elsewhere in this issue went on a specific comparison (`Awesome-LLMOps (InftyAI)` against
a twenty-times-larger CC0 twin). Nothing comparable applies here.

No eval file existed before this pass; this stub is the placeholder, not an evaluation.
